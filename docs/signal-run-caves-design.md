**Order:**

## The idea in one line
Hidden holes in the canyon drop you straight down into pitch-black natural caves that an old mine broke into. You explore with a flashlight, a jetpack and a glowing guideline. One of the 7 power cells is down there, and the story continues underground after all 7 dishes are fixed.

## Story hook
- One existing power cell moves underground: **"the head of the reservoir"** cell. Nothing else on the surface gets rebuilt.
- Its blue light beam (already 520 m tall) shines straight up out of the hidden sinkhole. That's the player's first clue that caves exist.
- Everything deeper is where the story goes after the 7th dish comes online. Lore: see `claude/signal-run-world-and-lore.md`. It centres on a research station on the deepest level.

## How the deep levels unlock (by circumstance, not a menu)
- **A sealed door (built in v8)** stands in the mine's first room, in front of the crystal cavern: a heavy colony research door with no power. When the 7th dish comes online, the restored array sends power down, and the door opens. (The original plan put it between Levels 2 and 3.)
- **A reward item on the surface:** at the same moment, a long **2 km survey reel** becomes available at the observatory. That's the line you need to go deep safely.
- Before all 7 dishes, players can explore Levels 1–2 with the short reels.

## Entrances (hidden, all tie together underground)
1. **Sinkhole**: a natural depression in the surface, easy to miss. Step off the ledge into the dark and drift down on the jetpack (~120 m). *Not built yet.*
2. **Old mine shaft near the dam**: a hole barely bigger than a person, with rotted timber around the rim, dropping straight down. *Built (v4): it drops about 190 m to the station, where the deep door is (v8).*
3. **Dam drainage pipe**: a concrete tunnel near the dam that slopes down and joins the mine. *Not built yet.*

All entrances drop below the deepest part of the canyon floor, so nothing on the surface can be seen from the caves.

## Levels (at least 4, each joined by drop shafts)
| Level | Kind | Rough tunnel length | What's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Mine workings | Built by people | ~2 km | Timber supports, rails, ore carts, lanterns; the spot where the mine broke into natural cave. **Built.** |
| 2. First caverns | Natural | ~3 km | The moved power cell, first big open chamber, the sealed door |
| 3. Crystal level | Natural | ~3 km | Glowing crystal cavern; tunnels split off falling round shafts and keep going deeper. **The cavern itself is built (under Level 1, see above).** |
| 4. Deep level | Natural + built | ~2 km | Abandoned research station (new story), maybe an underground lake |

- Total network: **about 9–10 km**. Tunnels 5–15 m wide, plus a few large open caverns.
- Looks: rough, uneven natural rock. Nothing boxy.

## Shape of the network: built for getting lost
- **Mostly branching, like a tree**: forks in the road, splits, side passages.
- **Hub rooms with 4–5 exits**, so you can leave a room the wrong way and not notice.
- **Very few loops**: only 2–3 spots on purpose where a tunnel circles back. Elsewhere, a wrong turn means backing out the way you came.
- Plenty of **dead ends and look-alike junctions**.
- Without a line you can genuinely get lost. **The line is a necessary mechanic**, not a nice extra.

## Light
- Pitch black underground. The flashlight is required.
- Daylight falls down each entrance shaft and fades the farther you go. Looking up while drifting down, the circle of sky shrinks. After the first bend, only your light is left.
- Crystals, the power cell and the guideline give off a faint glow of their own.
- The crystal cavern is the exception: its glowing life lights it enough to see across.

## Agreed additions (not built yet)
- **Flares**: limited number; throw one to light up a big space.
- **Scanner / inspection tool**: the story reason detail rises when you look closely.
- **A stationary light** in a different location (separate from the cavern).
- **Hazards for deeper levels**: magma (heat damage), acid vapour (timed exposure), oxygen.

## Flashlight & batteries
- The battery drains slowly. The light dims and flickers when it's low.
- Recharge **only by swapping in spare batteries** found in the caves. Refuel pads sometimes have a spare.
- The battery is the main limit underground.

## When the flashlight dies: the "you're in trouble" mechanic
Like a near-death mode in other games: it only happens when things have gone wrong, and it should feel that way.

In both modes, the guideline and the crystals keep glowing. **If you laid line, you can always follow it out.** The rules below are for when there's no line nearby.

**Easy mode: feel your way, then pass out** *(the pass-out is built; the "feeling" effects are not yet)*
- You get about **200 m of walking in the dark** before you pass out. That's enough to reach a line or battery you know is close behind or ahead.
- "Feeling" in the dark:
  - The screen is near black.
  - When you brush a wall, a faint outline of the rock appears around where you touched it, then fades.
  - On the phone, a short buzz when you bump rock.
  - Footsteps and breathing echo; a big room sounds different from a tight tunnel.
- As the 200 m runs out, the view narrows and the breathing gets heavier. Then a fade to black and a short message ("You got turned around in the dark…"). You wake up at the last refuel pad you touched.

**Hard mode: emergency sonar, no rescue** *(not built yet)*
- No passing out and no rescue. You have to get yourself out.
- **Sonar is emergency-only.** It switches on only when your flashlight is dead, and switches off the moment you have light again. It is never an exploring tool.
- Each ping sends out a ring that briefly shows the cave around you:
  - **No colour** and a coarse, low-detail look.
  - **Short range** (starting guess ~30 m), and it fades after a second or two.
  - Can't ping again for a few seconds.
- How it works (plain words): it reuses the cave drawing with a different paint rule. Everything is grey and lit only by the expanding ring. That makes it a fairly small job.

## Guideline (cave diver's line)
- Emergency backup for getting home.
- **Glows softly with no flashlight; bright and easy to see in the beam.**
- **Line stays in the cave** after you follow it back out.
- **New reels tie onto the end of old line**, the way cave divers do it, so your safe paths keep growing.
- **Direction arrows on the line point toward the way out** (as in real cave diving), so a lost player always knows which way is out.
- Heads-up warnings: **100 m left → 25 m left → "Line out."**
- **Reels (starting plan to tune):**
  - 750 m starter reel, found near the entrance *(built)*
  - 1 km reel on Level 2
  - 2 km survey reel at the observatory, given when all 7 dishes are online
  - 5 km reel on Level 4
- Levels 1–2 are ~5 km of tunnel with 1.75 km of line, so you can't line all of it before the deep levels open.

## Jetpack & fuel
- Jetpack works fully underground, even in 5 m tunnels. Ceilings stop you from going up.
- **Easy mode:** fuel refills whenever you're standing on ground (works as it does now).
- **Hard mode:** fuel refills **only on refuel pads**.
  - Rule so you never get stuck: **a pad at the bottom of every drop.** Falling is free and climbing costs fuel, so from anywhere you can walk downhill to a pad.
  - Surface pads: river valley floor by the dam (the lowest point), plus a few up top (observatory, lookout).
  - Underground pads: the bottom of each level, styled as old mine fuel drums or research-station power posts. The cavern's landing ledge needs one.

## Cameras
- Keep the current behind-the-character camera with zoom.
- **Add a first-person view** (see through the character's eyes). On the phone, one small camera button; on the laptop, a key such as V.
- The flashlight comes from the head. Fuel, battery and line warnings show the same in both views.
- Behind-the-character camera: pulls in close when rock gets in the way, so it never goes through tunnel walls *(built)*.

## How it runs (plain words)
- The caves are stored in the file as a **recipe, not finished triangles**, the same way the canyon is. The file only grows a little.
- Target: **~50 million triangles** of cave in total, built piece by piece as you get close to it. Only the nearby few million are drawn each frame. The crystal cavern draws about 1.4–1.6M triangles in its widest views.
- **Underground, the surface isn't drawn at all**, and above ground the caves aren't drawn. Each side gets the full budget, so both can be just as detailed.
- Physics built: ceilings, tunnel walls, camera pull-in, and cave-floor ground instead of the canyon surface when you're underground. Crystals don't block you yet.

## Phone screen
Keep it uncluttered:
- The existing stick and JET / BOOST / DIVE, plus one camera button.
- In hard mode, a PING button appears only when the flashlight is dead.
- Battery, line and humidity readouts sit small near the fuel bar.
- Warnings use the existing message popup.
