# Signal Run — world & lore (open ideas, nothing locked)

Status (23 Nov 2026): Christopher is **sitting with these ideas**. Treat everything here as a lens to build around, not settled canon, except the points marked **Decided**. Other possibilities stay welcome. He has also made another mining game whose ideas may feed in.

## Setting
- **Decided: one of hundreds of terraformed planets.** Humanity has partially terraformed many worlds. This one is a settler outpost, and **something went wrong at this outpost**.
- **Decided: something was unearthed.** That is the core of what went wrong.
  - Open: whether it pays off as **encountering beings** (or something like that). Christopher calls it a possibility, not a commitment.
- **Decided: the sky and water stay Earth-like**, because the air and water have been made Earth-like.
- **Decided: people lived and worked here without suits.** Combustion engines work. The outposts were normal working places.
- **Decided: airlocks, pressure seals and similar gear are standard settler safety equipment on every building**, the way anyone would build on a newly settled world. They are not retrofits.
- **Decided: the player wears a suit because something happened.** What happened is part of the mystery.
- **Decided: surface plants photosynthesise**, so they are greens and browns. Their *shapes* can be alien (umbrella canopies, fan pads, bulb-tipped stalks, bladders), but their colours are natural.
- **Underground biology can be stranger:** glowing life, unusual colours. That's where the alien palette (teal, violet, magenta, cyan) belongs.
- The player is an explorer visiting **old outposts**.
- The surface is "level one". **A big share of the game takes place underground.**

## Tone arc
- **Mostly wonder:** beautiful, mysterious, discovery.
- **Later-game eerie mystery, earned by doing everything.** Players who just explore never meet it. It begins once all the antennas are powered and the deeper underground areas open up.

## Ideas on the table (Christopher's, not locked)
- **Ancient, super-powerful aliens were here millions of years ago.** Near the crystal cavern there could be a site with **artifacts to find**, far older than the colony.
- **A partly flooded cavern** you can swim down into and look around underwater. A natural home for the eerie part.
- **A stalactite/stalagmite cavern.** More distinct cavern types, less plain tunnel.
- An option Claude raised: the colony's survey team found the alien site, which is why their camp in the crystal cavern looks abandoned in a hurry.

## What's built that carries story (v8)
- **The deep door "D-7"** in the old mine's first room: a heavy, modern colony door bolted into the rock, stencilled "SEALED".
  - Behind it, a short drift ends at a drop into the crystal cavern.
  - Its control panel is dead, and a cable runs from it back up the shaft to the surface.
  - In the campaign it gets power only when all 7 dishes are online. The game says "Far below, in the old mine, a dead door has power."
  - Implication (open): the research team sealed what was below and tied the door to the array.
- **The survey camp** on the cavern's landing ledge: deck, field lab, crates, instrument mast, core drill, fallen shelter. Left in a hurry.
- **The suit's humidity sensor**: 97% in the cavern, and the visor fogs.

- **The flooded cavern (v11)** holds the research team's **dive station**: a floating platform, air tanks, their line spool and a **dive log terminal**.
  - At the bottom of the lake is **the bore**: a perfectly round, glass-smooth tunnel with a ring cut every half metre. It is not natural and not human-made, and it is older than the rock is supposed to be. The team sealed it with D-10.
  - Their old guideline runs into the bore and ends **cut clean** at the door.
  - The lake's **fish** are big, pale and fast. They never attack, but they watch: eyes at the edge of the light, flaring in your lamp.

### Dive log (draft text, Christopher may rewrite any of it)
1. *DAY 1:* Lake is 31 m deep, 4 °C, visibility 8 m, sealed (no inflow, no current). Dives 1 and 2 clean.
2. *DAY 3:* "Something big passed under me at the 20 m line. Kel says it was my own bubbles… Bubbles don't swim against you."
3. *DAY 6:* Found the bore. Perfectly round, glass-smooth, rings every half metre. "Not water-cut. Not ours." Cores older than the rock should be.
4. *DAY 9:* "Eyes at the edge of the light. They follow the line back to the platform… All of us have seen them now."
5. *DAY 11:* Kel went into the bore alone. "His line runs forty metres in and stops. Cut clean. Not frayed. Cut."
6. *DAY 12:* "We are sealing D-10 in the bore, and D-9 behind us. Couplings pulled. If you are reading this, the array is listening again, and something has heard it. Do not dive alone. Do not follow the lights."

This draft ties together several open ideas: something unearthed, ancient non-human builders, the array and the signal, and the couplings. None of it is locked.

## Surface reskin (done in v5/v6)
- **Vehicles:** rugged outpost truck KR-07, scout buggy, cargo trailer.
- **Camp:** hab shelter, data table, survey scanner, heater beacon, comms mast, sealed cases.
- **Outpost buildings:** standard settler safety gear such as airlocks, radiators and masts.
- **Marina:** sampling landing, survey barge, drone skiff, instrument buoys.
- **Flyers:** violet four-winged creatures in place of ravens.
- **Plants:** alien shapes in greens and browns.
- **Left as colony infrastructure:** the dam, bridge, power lines, dishes and lookout tower.

## Mechanics the story should explain
- **Scanner:** the suit's survey tool. Close inspection reveals fine detail and records samples and log entries. It is the in-world reason things look more detailed up close.
- **Suit sensors:** humidity (built), temperature, air quality.
- **Oxygen / suit endurance** as a clock in hazard zones, and underwater in a flooded cavern.
- **Flares** (limited, found below or in old supply caches) to light large spaces.
- **A stationary light you switch on** somewhere (for example an old outpost's floodlights).
- **Headlamp battery and spare batteries** (built).
- **Guideline reels**, line arrows and warnings (built).
- Idea: **old guidelines left by earlier explorers**. Some lead out, some are cut or end at collapses.

## Places & hazards
- **Giant glowing crystal cavern** (built): the showcase, directly under the old mine's first room and reached through the deep door. Glowing life lights it so you can see across. It has ledges, cliffs, a pool, and five tunnels branching off.
- **Magma level:** heat damages you, which makes it timed.
- **Acidic vapour area:** also timed, even with the suit and oxygen.
- Existing Level 1: a mine shaft near the dam leading into timbered mine drifts, then natural caves (built in v4). The timber mine fits a settled, working colony.

## History layers (draft)
- The colonists' own mine broke into the glowing caverns.
- Something was unearthed. A research team studied it and sealed the way down behind the deep door.
- Something happened. People left, or didn't make it.
- The player now: sent first to restore the array, then to follow the signal below.

The draft hook still fits: **the array was never listening to the sky. The signal comes from underground.**
