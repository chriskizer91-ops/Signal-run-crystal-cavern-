# Signal Run: the crystal cavern build

Signal Run is a 3D exploration game in **one HTML file**. Open it in Chrome or Edge, on a laptop or a phone, and play. There are no images or downloads: every mountain, model, texture and sound is generated by the code in that file.

It started as a canyon rendering benchmark: 147 million triangles of terrain, a reservoir, a dam and a radio observatory. It became a game:
- **Surface:** the radio array is dark. Find 7 power cells and fly them back with your jetpack.
- **Underground:** an old mine shaft leads down into caves. You have a headlamp with a battery and a glowing guideline so you can find your way out. Below that, a series of giant caverns are stacked deeper and deeper, joined by sealed doors and drop shafts.

**Play it:** open [`game/signal-run.html`](game/signal-run.html) (v13) (download it, then open the file in your browser).

## Where things are

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `game/signal-run.html` | **The game.** The current version (v13). This one file is the real source. |
| `game/versions/` | Every version, from the original v2 up to v13, so nothing is lost. |
| `src/cavelib.js` | The cave system's code (shapes of all tunnels and caverns, floors, physics). An exact copy of the part inside the game, kept separately so it can be tested on its own. |
| `src/runtime/` | The code for the caves and each cavern (crystal, stalactite, flooded, giant crystal) as it was written before being added to the game. |
| `src/patches/` | The scripts that turned each version into the next (v4 caves, v7 crystal cavern, v8 door and modes, v9 stalactite cavern, v10 flight and trails, v11 flooded cavern, v12 giant crystal cavern, v13 dry tunnel). |
| `assets/modules/` | **Every 3D model**, one file per group: vehicles, camp, outpost buildings, marina, plants, power lines, bridge, crystals, glowing cave life, the survey camp, the sealed doors, the work lights and parts, the dripstone, the fish, the dive station, and the giant crystals and crystal carpet (`geode`). |
| `assets/modules/originals/` | The surface models as they were before the far-future reskin. |
| `assets/previews/` | Pictures of each model. |
| `assets/briefs/` | The instructions given to the helper agents that built the models. |
| `assets/*.js, *.py, preview.html` | The model toolkit and the tools to count triangles, take preview pictures and put models into the game. |
| `tests/` | The automatic test scripts (walk routes, open doors, take screenshots, compare versions). |
| `docs/` | The project notes: how the engine works, the cave design plan, and the world and lore. |
| `screenshots/` | In-game screenshots from each step. |

## Versions
- **v2:** the original game.
- **v3:** phone touch controls.
- **v4:** the mine shaft and Level 1 caves: headlamp, battery, guideline, passing out in the dark.
- **v5:** far-future reskin of the surface (rugged colony gear, alien plant shapes).
- **v6:** plants recoloured green and brown (they photosynthesise).
- **v7:** the giant glowing crystal cavern.
- **v8:**
  - The crystal cavern moved under the first cave room.
  - The sealed door D-7 now leads to it.
  - The menu has **Campaign** and **Free play** modes.
- **v9:**
  - The **stalactite cavern** below the crystal cavern, reached through door D-8.
  - Work floodlights by each deeper door.
  - In the campaign, a power coupling to find for each deeper door.
- **v10:**
  - Steadier flying: you stop when you let go, it's slower in caves, and you hover when you let go of JET (hold DIVE to go down).
  - Glowing route stakes lead from each cavern's landing to its exit door.
- **v11:** the **flooded cavern** behind door D-9.
  - A black underground lake, with swimming and suit air.
  - Big pale fish you only glimpse, with eyes that shine in your lamp.
  - The research team's abandoned dive station and their dive log.
  - A strange smooth tunnel at the bottom of the lake, sealed with door D-10.
- **v12:** the **giant crystal cavern**, the finale, through D-10 and up the bore.
  - A geode 260 m across and 210 m tall, its walls banded like agate and carpeted with 16,000 small crystals.
  - 34 giant crystals up to 104 m long that you can see into: light bends through them, they glow from a bright core, and ghost outlines of their younger selves show inside.
  - Their own light colours the rock, the water, the air and you.
  - You surface in a clear pool. The cavern is dim until a slow pulse of light rings out from the pool and wakes it.
  - In the campaign, D-10's coupling lies on the lake floor, and a spare tank on the dive station's rack doubles your air.
  - You can stand on the giant crystals; they're solid.
- **v13:** a new way into the giant crystal cavern (Christopher ran out of air in v12's long flooded tunnel).
  - Past D-10 the tunnel climbs up out of the water. You swim about 12 s after the door, then walk up a dry tunnel.
  - It ends at a balcony high in the cavern's wall, 38 m above the water. You step out, and the pulse wakes the cavern in front of you.
  - Measured without the spare tank: 32 s under water in all, out of 75 s of air.

## Quick shortcuts (add to the end of the game's address)
- `?play=freenew`: new free play game (everything unlocked).
- `?play=new`: new campaign.
- `&at=door`, `&at=cavern`, `&at=d8`, `&at=drip`, `&at=d9`, `&at=lake`, `&at=d10`: start next to door D-7, in the crystal cavern, at door D-8, in the stalactite cavern, at door D-9, on the lake's beach, or at door D-10 under water.
- `&at=ledge`, `&at=pool`, `&at=geo`: start on the giant crystal cavern's balcony, in its pool, or on its floor beside the pool.
- `&geoq=lo` or `&geoq=hi`: force the phone or laptop version of the giant cavern (normally picked automatically: touch screens get the lighter one).
